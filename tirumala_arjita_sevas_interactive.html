How to read this guide

  • Ticket admits (persons per ticket) is how many pilgrims a single seva ticket covers — not a party-size cost. A "1 person" seva issues one ticket per pilgrim; a "2 persons" seva admits two on one ticket (typically a couple, e.g. Kalyanotsavam, Vratham, Veda Ashirvachanam). Use the Ticket admits switch to see only sevas that match how many people you want on one ticket. The cost shown is the fixed ticket price from TTD.
  • When available tells you the frequency and, for seasonal sevas, the festival or month: Daily offered most days · Weekly on a fixed weekday (e.g. Padmavathi Abhishekam is a Friday seva) · Monthly on a lunar day (Pournami / Amavasya / Punarvasu) · Annual / Seasonal only in a named month or festival (Dhanurmasam = mid-Dec–mid-Jan, Thaimasam = mid-Jan–mid-Feb, Vasanthotsavam = Mar–Apr spring, Pavitrotsavam = Aug–Sep, Brahmotsavam = Sep–Oct, Float Festival = Feb–Mar, Jyeshthabhishekam = May–Jun) · Occasional grand / special-booking days.
  • Frequency & weekday are derived from the live TTD Arjitha-Seva booking calendar (the slot-availability captured across Aug–Nov 2026) combined with the temple festival calendar. A seva that only opened on Fridays in the booking window is shown as a weekly Friday seva. Always re-confirm exact dates and the current year's festival schedule on the official portal before travel.
  • Amounts, reporting location, reporting time and prasadam (privileges) are exactly as published in the TTD Arjitha-Seva master (ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in). Report at least 30–45 minutes before the stated time with original photo ID.
