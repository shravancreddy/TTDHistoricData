Official eDIP / Arjitha Seva Booking Rules & Eligibility

      Reading This Guide

      • Cost shown updates with your party size. Each seva ticket admits a fixed number of pilgrims (the "per-ticket" figure). If your party is larger than one ticket allows, additional tickets are added and the total is recalculated automatically.
      • Couple sevas (e.g. Kalyanotsavam, Vratham, Veda Ashirvachanam) are performed by one married couple per ticket and admit 2 pilgrims — a party of 2 needs only 1 ticket.
      • Frequency is inferred from each seva's name and TTD's ritual calendar: Daily offered most days · Weekly on a fixed weekday (e.g. Friday/Saturday) · Monthly on lunar days (Pournami/Amavasya) · Annual / Seasonal only during festivals or sacred months (Brahmotsavam, Dhanurmasam, Thaimasam, Pavitrotsavam, Vasanthotsavam, etc.).
      • Reporting time & location are exactly as published on the TTD Arjitha Seva master. Report at least 30–45 minutes before the stated time with original photo ID.
      • Amounts, timings and prasadam (privileges) are sourced from the TTD Arjitha Seva master data (ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in). Always re-confirm current-year dates and availability on the official portal before travel.

      Official Portals & Helpline

      • Arjita Seva & Darshan booking: ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in & the Govinda mobile app.
      • Donor / Srivani darshans & eDonation: tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in. Information hub: www.tirumala.org.
      • TTD Helpline (24×7 toll-free): 1800-425-333333 · 1800-425-4141 · 155257. TTD does not authorise any private agents — beware of touts and fraudulent websites.
