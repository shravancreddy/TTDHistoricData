General Notes, Restrictions & Reminders

  • Children below 12 years free on all categories with parent/guardian. Always carry age proof (birth certificate / school ID). Children 12+ need their own ticket.
  • ID Proof mandatory for all adults: Aadhaar (Indians) / Passport (NRI/Foreign). Tickets are biometrically linked and non-transferable on SED, Divya Darshan, and all Arjita Seva categories.
  • Dress Code (confirmed from TTD source): Male: Dhoti, Shirt / Female: Saree / Half Saree / Chudidar with Dupatta. Western casuals not permitted near sanctum, queue complexes, or Mada Streets. Footwear not permitted inside queue lines, Mada Streets, or temple.
  • Mahadwaram (East Gate) is the sacred inner temple gateway visible to pilgrims. It is not a public entry point from outside. No pilgrim category (including VVIP) uses Mahadwaram as an entry gate. All pilgrims enter through VQC / Supatham / designated protocol lanes.
  • SED ₹300 General Darshan: 90-day cooling period between bookings per person. Arjita Seva eDIP: 180-day cooling period between registrations (confirmed from TTD booking portal JS source).
  • Virtual Seva is separate from SED — it is its own booking flow (virtual-seva / connected darshan) and uses a different queue lane at VQC-1. Do not confuse with the standard ₹300 SED ticket.
  • Vaikunta Dwara Darshanam dates change each year — always verify current year's exact dates at TTD website before planning travel.
  • Supatham Entry is shared by: Senior Citizens, Divyang, Medical cases, Infant parents, NRIs, Newly Wed couples, SRIVANI donors, Donor quota holders. Timing approximately 12 PM – 6 PM for walk-in categories. Confirm current timings with TTD.
  • VIP Break Darshan (L3 / recommendation letter) is suspended during Brahmotsavam (~9 days Sept–Oct) and certain major utsavams. Check blackout dates at TTD website before travelling.
  • Rescheduling / Cancellation: Not allowed for any online-booked category (SED, Arjita Seva, Senior Citizen, Virtual Seva). Late arrival = slot forfeited, no refund.
  • Quota release schedule (approx. 3 months ahead): SED ₹300 → 22nd at 10 AM · Arjita Seva eDIP reg → 18th–21st at 10 AM · Angapradakshinam → 21st at 10 AM · Senior/Divyang → 23rd at 3 PM · Virtual Seva PM release → 21st at 3 PM · Accommodation → 24th · SRIVANI Trust advance quota → 23rd at 11 AM · SRIVANI current-day → daily at 9 AM IST.
  • Official booking portals: SED, Arjita Seva, Accommodation → ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in & Govinda app. Srivani / Donor darshans / eDonation → tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in. Info hub → www.tirumala.org.
  • TTD Helpline (24×7 toll-free): 1800-425-333333 · 1800-425-4141 · 155257. TTD does not authorise any agents for darshan or seva booking. Beware of fraudulent websites and touts.
